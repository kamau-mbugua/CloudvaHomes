How it works

From move-in to landlord payout, one continuous ledger.

No spreadsheets, no manual receipt books. Each step feeds the next, so the numbers in your reports are the numbers in M-Pesa.

  1. 01 · Move in

    Add units and tenants

    Set rent, deposits and move-in fees per unit. Tenancies keep the full history.

  2. 02 · Bill

    Run invoices in one click

    Rent, water and power for a whole property, with meter photos as proof.

  3. 03 · Collect

    Tenants pay how they like

    Paybill, Till, an STK prompt from the portal, bank transfer or cash.

  4. 04 · Reconcile

    Payments find their invoice

    Matched by account reference or phone, allocated oldest-due first.

  5. 05 · Remit

    Pay the owner

    Commission and expenses deducted, net sent to the landlord by M-Pesa B2C.

Reconciliation

Month-end close in minutes, not a weekend.

Every shilling that arrives is logged as it lands, then matched and allocated. Your team only handles the exceptions.

  • Matched on reference or phone. Unit numbers and registered phone numbers both work.
  • Oldest balance cleared first. Part payments and overpayments are handled correctly.
  • Nothing silently disappears. Unmatched payments wait in a queue until someone assigns them.

FAQ

Questions landlords and hosts ask us

Something else on your mind? Write to hello@kodihq.co.ke.

Which payment methods can tenants use? +

M-Pesa Paybill and Till, an STK Push prompt sent from the tenant portal, bank transfers through statement imports, plus cash and cheque recorded by your staff. All of them land in the same ledger.

Do I pay for vacant units? +

No. You are billed per occupied unit each month, with a small monthly minimum. Empty units cost nothing.

How does automatic M-Pesa matching work? +

When a payment arrives, KodiHQ reads the account reference and the payer's phone number, finds the tenant and clears their oldest unpaid invoice first. Payments it cannot place go to a review queue.

Is KodiHQ suitable for letting agencies? +

Yes. Agencies set up management agreements per landlord, earn commission automatically, deduct approved expenses and remit the net to each owner, who gets a read-only portal.

Can I run short-stay BNB units on KodiHQ? +

Yes. Sign up as a short-stay host and you get your own booking site at yourname.kodihq.co.ke. Guests pick dates, see photos and pay you by M-Pesa, with no app or account needed. You run the calendar, cleaning, guests and profit per unit from the web and mobile apps.

How are short-stay hosts billed? +

At the same rate, counted per published listing each month. Listings you unpublish cost nothing. Guest payments go straight to your own Paybill or Till, and KodiHQ never holds them.

Can caretakers use it from their phone? +

Yes. The Android and iOS app lets caretakers capture meter readings with photos, update unit status and handle move-ins and move-outs, without access to money settings.

Can I bring my existing tenants and balances? +

Yes. Import your units, tenants and opening balances in bulk via CSV, or let our team help your organisation set up properties, units, tenants and opening balances during the free trial.
