3. ACCOUNT DELETION
======================================================================
Delete account URL: https://app.kodihq.co.ke/delete-account
  - Public page. Signed out, it explains how to delete and links to sign-in.
    Signed in, it is the delete form itself.
  - People who can't sign in can email privacy@cloudvalabs.com.
  - NOTE FOR THE OWNER: deploy the latest web app to production before entering
    this URL; the live site did not have the page yet on 2026-11-23.

In the app:
  - Account menu (avatar) > Delete account.
  - Tenants also: More > My profile > "Delete my account".
  - Confirmed with a one-time code sent to the account's phone or email.

What is deleted:
  - At once: sign-in details (email, phone, password); every session ends.
  - After 30 days: the rest of the account (name, old contact details,
    sessions, sign-in codes, in-app notifications).

What is kept:
  - Records landlords, agencies and hosts must keep (invoices, receipts,
    payments, bookings) stay with those businesses, as the law requires.

Play's "partial deletion" question: users can also ask privacy@cloudvalabs.com
for a copy of their data, or for it to be erased sooner.

9. DATA SAFETY
======================================================================
Collects data: yes
Shares data with third parties: no
  Service providers acting for us count as processing, not sharing:
  Africa's Talking (SMS), our email provider, Safaricom M-Pesa (payments),
  hosting.
Encrypted in transit: yes (HTTPS only)
Users can request deletion: yes, in the app and at the URL in section 3

Data types (type | collected | required? | purposes):

- Name
  Collected. Required. App functionality, account management.
- Email address
  Collected. Optional (email OR phone). App functionality, account management,
  fraud prevention/security (sign-in codes).
- Phone number
  Collected. Optional (email OR phone). Same as email, plus M-Pesa payment
  prompts.
- Address (the rented home or unit, from the landlord's records)
  Collected. Required for tenants. App functionality.
- Purchase history (rent invoices, payments, receipts, bookings)
  Collected. Required. App functionality.
- Other financial info (balances, deposits, landlord payout details)
  Collected. Required. App functionality.
- User payment info (card or bank numbers)
  NOT collected. M-Pesa is used; no card numbers are handled.
- Photos
  Collected. Optional. App functionality (meter readings, maintenance,
  listings, inspections).
- Files and docs
  Collected. Optional. App functionality (receipts, attachments).
- Other in-app messages / user-generated content
  Collected. Optional. App functionality (maintenance requests, notices).
- Device or other IDs
  NOT collected in this build. If push notifications are turned on later (FCM
  token), change to: collected, optional, app functionality.
- Location, contacts, calendar, health, audio, web history, app activity
  analytics, crash logs
  NOT collected.

None of this data is used for advertising or sold. A small amount is processed
for security: sign-in codes and an audit log of account changes.

======================================================================
10. FINANCIAL FEATURES DECLARATION
======================================================================
Leave the final answer to the owner and Legal. What the app does:
  - Shows tenants their rent, invoices, receipts and statements.
  - Starts M-Pesa payments (STK push) for rent and bookings, to the landlord's
    or host's OWN paybill or till, through Safaricom.
  - Does NOT sell KodiHQ's own services in the app: in the Play build, SMS
    bundles and the KodiHQ subscription bill can't be paid in the app, and the
    app does not link to or explain other ways to pay for them (businesses pay
    on the web).
  - KodiHQ does not hold user funds, offer loans or credit, or deal in crypto.
